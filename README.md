A tool to test cell internal resistance and bus bar resistance. You can send the results to us when you order your custom BMS firmware. 

Calibrating bus bar resistance removes the high cell voltage drop under load, improves range by not triggering turtle mode or 0% SOC early, improves fast charge speed, remove regen surge and reports true cell health.

Perform this test at 80% SOC, with battery temperature around 25-30 degrees C. The longer you run the test, the more accurate the results will be
